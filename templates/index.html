How to Play Checkers

Objective: Capture all of your opponent's pieces or block them so they cannot move.

  • Movement: Pieces move diagonally forward, one square at a time.
  • Capturing: Jump over an opponent's piece diagonally to capture it. You must land on an empty square.
  • Mandatory Captures: If a capture is available, you must take it!
  • Multiple Jumps: If after capturing you can capture again, you must continue jumping.
  • Kings: When a piece reaches the opposite end of the board, it becomes a King (marked with a crown). Kings can move and capture both forward and backward.

Controls: Drag and drop your pieces, or click to select and click again to move.
