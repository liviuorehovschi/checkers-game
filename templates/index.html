How to Play

Objective: Capture all opponent pieces or block them from moving.

1

Movement: Pieces move diagonally forward one square at a time.

2

Capturing: Jump over an opponent's piece diagonally to capture it.

!

Mandatory Captures: If a capture is available, you must take it.

3

Multi-jumps: If another capture is available after jumping, you must continue.

Queens: Reach the opposite end to become a Queen. Queens move in all diagonal directions.

Controls: Drag and drop, or tap to select then tap destination.
