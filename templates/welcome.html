Liviu's Checkers

Many online checkers games miss a core rule: mandatory captures. If you can capture, you must. This implementation gets that right, along with proper king promotion and multi-jump sequences.

Built for the Problem Solving with AI class at Minerva University. The AI uses the minimax algorithm with alpha-beta pruning.

Supports both Player vs AI and Player vs Player modes. Works on desktop and mobile.
